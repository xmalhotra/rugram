## Archived legacy prompt (not the current generation specification)

Viewer control: a deliberate right or left tilt starts timed playback in that direction (1.2 seconds end-to-end). An independent animation clock continues to the endpoint even if no more sensor samples arrive; only a deliberate opposite tilt reverses it. Poses advance one at a time without endpoint wrapping. Recenter changes the neutral angle without interrupting playback. Slider mode stops automatic playback and permits manual inspection of a middle pose. Playback waits for the image to load. Motion subscriptions and animation clocks stop when the movement is not active or the app leaves the foreground.

There is one fixed-height position row beside the pose count. Tilt uses the segmented indicator; Slider replaces that indicator in the same space with a range track and marked frame stops. There is no additional slider/status row and no layout change when switching modes. Tapping a stop or dragging selects a frame. Motion retry/status lives under ⓘ.

Successful motion startup/resume is silent: no “Tilt on” status is emitted. Only actionable diagnostics are shown under ⓘ. Swiping closes transient information and clips each page to keep neighboring content from leaking into the card transition.

Every cell has the identical plain dark charcoal-green studio background, fixed camera side/three-quarter side view, entire human including hands and feet fully inside each cell with generous margin, realistic human anatomy, natural athletic proportions and skin texture. Same adult athlete, scale, clothing, camera and ground level in every cell. Render a high-quality realistic 3D human demonstrator, not stick figures. Each row shows four consecutive small phases of ONE movement from left to right; frames must move limbs, not merely rotate a frozen pose. Row 1 bear crawl: bent knees hovering just above floor, neutral back; alternating opposite hand/foot short controlled steps. Row 2 crab walk: face-up supported on palms and flat feet with bent knees, low comfortable hip lift; short alternating hand/foot steps. Row 4 frog travel: squat, hands reach forward, weight transfers to hands, feet follow to a squat without high jumping. Row 5 inchworm walkout: standing hip hinge with soft knees, palms floor and hands walk out, controlled plank, hands walk back toward feet; no pushup. Row 6 bird dog: all-fours setup, one arm and opposite leg extend low in line with torso, return all-fours, opposite arm and leg extend. All exercises calm controlled technique, joints anatomically plausible, no contortions, no extra/missing limbs, clothing covers torso and hips, no equipment. No claims of clinical accuracy.
