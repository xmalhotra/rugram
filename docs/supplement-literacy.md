# Supplement literacy: magnesium first

Available in Nutrition → Understand supplements → Magnesium, and Picks → Supplements → Compare magnesium forms. The same guide component and central version-controlled data back both entry points. The Nutrition version can be added to Home as a learning flow; it never adds a supplement-taking schedule.

Three progressive screens:

1. Compare two forms. Citrate, oxide and chloride have qualified small-human-study summaries with original references. Glycinate/bisglycinate is explicitly **not assessed in this initial review**, not declared ineffective or inferior. No universal winner, numeric absorption score, invented percentages or whole-brand gold badge.
2. Read actual label information. An optional calculator takes a user-entered pack price, servings per pack and elemental magnesium per serving. It calculates price per 100 mg of label-declared elemental content; this is neither an absorbed amount nor a suggested dose. User-entered data is not represented as independently verified. Blends with undisclosed ratios stay unknown.
3. Separate absorption evidence, goal-specific outcome evidence and creator experience. Show safety context and original source links. The app does not assess deficiency, prescribe treatment or promise improved sleep.

The initial form summaries use NIH ODS, the Firoz & Graber human comparison and NCCIH’s magnesium/sleep summary, consulted 2026-10-15. These sources support specific statements, not endorsement of the guide or every product containing a form. This is an editorial evidence summary, not a completed clinical review. No Fort Pharma content or endorsement is attributed without the user’s source.

There are deliberately no newly invented magnesium products or current brand prices. Linking brands requires the exact product/variant, label source and date, declared forms and elemental amount, serving size, current price reference and independent product-quality evidence when claimed. Creator citations and sponsorship disclosures remain separate from scientific sources. Product-label ingestion, persistence, admin editing and brand-to-form joins are not implemented in this increment; the form taxonomy is currently centralized in `shared/magnesium.ts` rather than a new database table.

Medical-content review, accessibility and physical-device testing are required before public release. Price arithmetic and data semantics have automated tests; this does not certify supplement safety for an individual.
