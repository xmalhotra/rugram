# Customer catalogue, 16 October 2026

Picks opens with a photo-led featured shelf and illustrated categories. Product previews show a model, variant and seller-price link; longer explanations, safety notes, specifications and sources remain in detail views. Supplement products can be opened without completing an educational deck.

Two additional exact-variant products were checked against the Sony authorised ShopAtSC store feed: WH-CH520 Black at ₹2,990 and ULT FIELD 1 Orange at ₹9,990. These are price/availability snapshots, not live price guarantees. Purchase records retain seller URLs, checked dates and variant IDs. No independent testing, influencer usage or review scores are invented.

Customer browsing excludes demo, archived, catalogue-only, missing-photo/variant and entirely out-of-stock entries. Family-level laptop listings and unavailable television listings remain research records rather than pretending to be ready-to-buy picks. This intentionally leaves some categories without consumer-ready products until exact variants are verified.

Admin controls product records, photos, purchase options and publication status. App settings `featuredProductIds` controls the featured shelf. Existing editorial data is preserved. The credit-card research directory remains in Admin, separate from the reviewed consumer cards.
