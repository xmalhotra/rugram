# Decision-led Picks

Inspiration: VMone's public comparison and sale-buying guides, not an endorsement or copied review. Source: https://t.me/s/VM_ONE1?before=1140. YouTube video bodies were not available through the text reader; the public channel posts and linked video descriptions were readable.

Product detail uses three swipe pages: who it suits; why consider it / key specifications / one trade-off; a same-budget alternative or a save/seller action. Full evidence stays behind All details. The public catalogue quality gate also applies to alternative candidates.

For electronics, choose one nearest-priced candidate in the same category, subtype and comparison group within 30% of the current product's recorded seller price. This is deterministic candidate matching, not an assertion of superior quality, brand reputation or a best-buy rank. Show a maximum of three short common specification rows. Missing comparable information remains explicitly unverified. Both product photos, seller prices and outbound links are visible. Manufacturer figures are not hands-on testing.

Food and supplement products do not use this price matching: pack sizes, doses and serving costs require normalization first. Existing alternatives remain accessible without declaring a price/value winner.

Editorial `assessment.bestFor`, `whyChosen`, `tradeoffs` and `specifications` remain managed product fields in Admin. Short UI excerpts do not delete the full information.
